# Career Goals – Tonu James

## Professional Career Objective

Tonu James is an experienced software engineer transitioning into Artificial Intelligence engineering. With nearly nine years of enterprise software development experience and an MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence from Technological University Dublin, his primary career objective is to establish himself in a professional AI engineering role that combines his software engineering background with modern Artificial Intelligence technologies.

Tonu is currently based in Ireland and actively seeking opportunities to restart his professional career in the technology industry, particularly in roles involving the practical development, integration, and deployment of intelligent software systems.

His professional interests span three complementary domains: Generative AI and LLM Engineering, Computer Vision and Edge AI, and Enterprise AI applications, including SAP-related Generative AI solutions. Tonu is equally open to opportunities across these areas and does not currently prioritize one specialization exclusively over the others.

## Target Professional Roles

Tonu is interested in AI engineering opportunities that allow him to apply his existing technical experience while continuing to develop practical expertise in modern AI systems.

### Generative AI and LLM Engineering

Tonu is interested in opportunities involving the development of applications powered by Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and agentic AI.

His learning and project interests include document retrieval, semantic search, embedding models, vector databases, prompt engineering, LLM integration, agentic workflows, and AI application evaluation.

He aims to develop the engineering expertise required to build, deploy, maintain, and improve reliable Generative AI applications.

Relevant roles include Generative AI Engineer, LLM Application Engineer, AI Engineer, and Agentic AI Developer.

### Computer Vision and Edge AI Engineering

Computer Vision represents another important area of Tonu's professional interest, supported by his academic background in Image Processing, MSc research in pedestrian detection, and practical experience with object detection applications.

Tonu has explored computer vision applications involving YOLO, OpenCV, PyTorch, ONNX Runtime, GStreamer, Docker, and Raspberry Pi-based Edge AI systems.

He is interested in developing intelligent vision applications for practical scenarios such as automated visual inspection, object detection, video analytics, and intelligent monitoring.

Relevant career opportunities include Computer Vision Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, and Edge AI Engineer.

### Enterprise AI and SAP Generative AI

Tonu is also interested in opportunities that combine Artificial Intelligence with enterprise software systems.

Having worked at SAP Labs India for nearly eight years, he has experience in enterprise application development, SAP technologies, customer-oriented software solutions, integration, and application support.

He recognizes the potential of Generative AI to enhance enterprise applications through intelligent automation, information retrieval, natural language interfaces, and AI-assisted business workflows.

As part of his professional development, Tonu is preparing for the SAP Certified – SAP Generative AI Developer (C_AIG) certification.

He is interested in opportunities involving Enterprise AI Engineering, SAP-related Generative AI development, and intelligent business application integration.

## Current Technical Development Goals

Tonu is actively expanding his practical capabilities in Generative AI, agentic AI, Computer Vision, Edge AI, and production-oriented software engineering.

His current learning and development activities include building RAG-powered applications, exploring LLM-based workflows, developing Raspberry Pi-based computer vision systems, and studying modern approaches to AI application deployment.

As part of his structured learning, Tonu is pursuing the courses "AI Engineer Production Track: Deploy LLMs & Agents at Scale" and "AI FDE: The Forward Deployed Engineer & Agentic AI Architect."

## Interest in Production AI Engineering

An important professional goal for Tonu is to understand how Artificial Intelligence solutions can be developed and deployed as reliable, maintainable, and scalable software systems.

His previous enterprise software engineering experience provides a foundation in software development practices, application integration, troubleshooting, and working with business-oriented systems.

He aims to extend this foundation with skills in AI system architecture, model integration, LLM application evaluation, API development, containerization, deployment automation, monitoring, and production engineering practices.

Tonu is particularly interested in understanding the complete lifecycle of AI applications, from identifying a problem and selecting suitable technologies to implementation, testing, deployment, and continuous improvement.

## Interest in Forward Deployed Engineering

Tonu is also exploring Forward Deployed Engineering as a possible career direction within Applied AI.

He is interested in understanding how AI engineers can work closely with business teams and customers to identify practical problems, design appropriate AI solutions, integrate them with existing software systems, and deliver measurable business value.

This interest connects his earlier enterprise development experience with his current learning in Generative AI, AI solution architecture, and agentic application development.

Forward Deployed Engineering is an emerging area of his professional development rather than a role he has previously held.

## Human-Centred and Responsible AI

Tonu's professional outlook is influenced by his MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence and his academic research into fairness and geographical bias in pedestrian detection systems.

He recognizes that the successful application of AI requires more than technical model performance. Reliability, fairness, explainability, transparency, and consideration of human needs are also important aspects of intelligent system development.

He aims to incorporate these principles into his approach to designing and evaluating AI-based applications.

## Long-Term Professional Vision

Tonu's long-term ambition is to become a versatile AI engineering professional who can combine strong software engineering practices with practical expertise in modern Artificial Intelligence technologies.

Rather than limiting his future career to a single AI specialization, he remains open to opportunities across Generative AI, Computer Vision, Edge AI, Enterprise AI, and related Applied AI engineering disciplines.

His objective is to contribute to the development of meaningful AI solutions that solve real-world problems, integrate effectively with existing software systems, and deliver practical value to users and organizations.

Tonu views continuous learning, hands-on experimentation, responsible AI development, and strong engineering practices as essential elements of his long-term professional growth.
