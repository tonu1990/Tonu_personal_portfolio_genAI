# Career Journey – Tonu James

## Early Career and Enterprise Software Development

Tonu James is an experienced software engineer with nearly nine years of professional experience in enterprise software development. His career began in December 2014 when he joined Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) as an Associate Developer.

During his time at TCS, from December 2014 to November 2015, Tonu worked on a project for Credit Suisse Investment Bank. His responsibilities included developing interactive reports involving large volumes of live-streaming financial data for banking risk calculations, primarily using Java.

In December 2015, Tonu joined SAP Labs India as a Software Developer, where he worked until August 2023. During this period, he gained extensive experience in enterprise software development, particularly within the SAP ecosystem.

His professional responsibilities included developing and supporting SAP CRM applications, working with SAP ABAP, CRM Web UI, BRF+, and OData-based integrations. He contributed to enterprise solutions for major clients, including Royal Bank of Canada, Standard Bank of South Africa, and Tetra Pak.

Tonu also gained leadership experience at SAP Labs, leading a four-member team involved in supporting more than 100 customers using the SAP Multi-Bank Connectivity (MBC) Connector.

## Early Interest in Artificial Intelligence

Tonu's interest in Artificial Intelligence developed gradually, beginning with his academic background in Image Processing during his M.Tech studies at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) between 2012 and 2014.

His postgraduate education introduced him to advanced image processing techniques and concepts related to artificial neural networks. His research during this period also involved video steganography, encryption, and information hiding, resulting in a research publication in 2014.

Although his subsequent professional career primarily involved enterprise software development, Tonu maintained an interest in emerging technologies, particularly Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

While working at SAP Labs, he began exploring AI technologies through self-directed learning and practical proof-of-concept projects. These projects involved technologies and approaches such as computer vision, few-shot learning, YOLO-based object detection, and autoencoders.

This hands-on exploration allowed him to connect his existing software engineering experience with the emerging possibilities of AI-based applications. His growing interest in intelligent systems encouraged him to explore the field beyond traditional enterprise software development.

## Decision to Pursue Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence

As Tonu continued exploring AI technologies through self-learning and practical experiments, he recognized the value of gaining a more comprehensive and structured understanding of Artificial Intelligence.

His earlier academic exposure to Image Processing and artificial neural networks, together with his AI-related proof-of-concept work at SAP Labs, influenced his decision to pursue formal postgraduate education in the field.

Importantly, Tonu wanted to understand not only the technical aspects of Artificial Intelligence but also its implications for people, society, fairness, ethics, and responsible technology development.

This combination of technical curiosity and interest in responsible AI motivated him to pursue an MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence at Technological University Dublin (TU Dublin), Ireland.

In September 2023, Tonu moved to Ireland to begin the programme, which he successfully completed in February 2025.

His postgraduate studies covered Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Statistics, Data Analysis, Human-Centred AI, and ethical considerations surrounding intelligent systems.

His master's research focused on fairness and geographical bias in pedestrian detection systems, comparing the performance of CNN-based pedestrian detectors under Indian and European conditions.

During his MSc studies, Tonu also worked part-time as a Software Development Lab Assistant at TU Dublin between March and May 2024, supporting undergraduate students in practical Python programming and Object-Oriented Programming exercises.

## Career Interruption and Renewed Professional Focus

Following the completion of his MSc in February 2025, Tonu's planned transition into AI-related employment was temporarily delayed by family caregiving responsibilities in India.

During this period, he travelled between Ireland and India to support his family, which limited the time he could dedicate to technical upskilling and searching for new professional opportunities.

Having returned to Ireland, Tonu is now actively working toward restarting his professional career and transitioning into AI engineering.

## Current Professional Development

Tonu is currently strengthening his practical engineering skills in Computer Vision, Edge AI, Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), agentic AI, and production-oriented AI systems.

His recent practical development activities include Raspberry Pi-based Edge AI applications using YOLO, GStreamer, ONNX Runtime, Python, and Docker.

He is also exploring the design and development of RAG-powered applications, including an AI chatbot integrated into his personal professional portfolio.

As part of his ongoing learning, Tonu is studying production-oriented LLM and agent deployment, as well as Forward Deployed Engineering and agentic AI architecture.

He is additionally preparing for the SAP Certified – SAP Generative AI Developer (C_AIG) certification, aiming to connect his previous SAP enterprise development experience with modern Generative AI capabilities.

## Career Direction

Tonu's current professional objective is to transition into AI engineering roles where he can combine his enterprise software development experience, academic background in Artificial Intelligence, and growing practical expertise in modern AI technologies.

His areas of interest include Applied Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Generative AI, RAG-based systems, agentic applications, and production-oriented AI engineering.

Rather than beginning a completely new technical career, Tonu is building upon his established software engineering foundation to develop the capabilities needed for modern AI engineering roles.
