# About Tonu James

Tonu James is an experienced software engineer transitioning into Artificial Intelligence engineering. Originally from Kerala, India, and currently based in Ireland, he brings nearly nine years of professional experience in enterprise software development, primarily with Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) and SAP Labs. His professional journey reflects a growing interest in applying Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to solve real-world problems.

Tonu holds a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, an M.Tech specializing in Image Processing, and an MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence from Technological University Dublin, Ireland, which he completed in February 2025. His academic and research interests include Computer Vision, Deep Learning, AI fairness, and the responsible development of intelligent systems.

Currently, Tonu is actively strengthening his expertise in Computer Vision, Edge AI, Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and agentic AI. His practical learning and development activities include Raspberry Pi-based Edge AI applications, intelligent software systems, and AI-powered chatbot development.

His professional goal is to combine his established enterprise software engineering experience with modern AI technologies to design, develop, and deploy reliable, scalable, and useful AI applications.

On a personal note, Tonu comes from a family rooted in Kerala. He is married and a father of three children.
